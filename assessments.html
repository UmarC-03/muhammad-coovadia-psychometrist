FET Phase Stream Alignment

Grade 9 Subject Choice Assessment

Informed FET phase subject stream selection for high school success

Target: Grade 9 Learners
Format: In-Person Paper Battery
Private Practice Rate (Itemised)

The transition into the Further Education and Training (FET) phase (Grades 10–12) requires learners to choose specific subjects that shape their future matric pass and university admission point scores (APS). This assessment battery evaluates cognitive aptitudes, intrinsic academic interests, and problem-solving reasoning to ensure subject selections align with the learner's genuine strengths and long-term potential.

Diagnostic Evaluation Dimensions

Cognitive Aptitude & Intellectual Strengths

Standardized objective measures assessing verbal, numerical, and abstract reasoning capacities.

Academic & Vocational Interests

Diagnostic profiling of occupational preferences, scientific vs. commercial inclinations, and creative orientations.

Curriculum Alignment

Evaluation of inductive and deductive thinking styles applicable across Mathematics vs Maths Literacy, Sciences, and Humanities.

Deliverables Included

  • Standardized paper-based testing battery administered in a quiet, structured environment
  • Objective statistical scoring and psychometric norm comparison
  • Detailed written evaluation report with concrete subject stream recommendations
  • Dedicated 1-on-1 in-person feedback consultation with learner and parents / guardians
Preparation Policy

No pre-assessment questionnaires or school reports are required beforehand.

Itemised invoice issued post-session for medical aid reimbursement submission.

Matric & Post-School Direction

Tertiary / University Field Selection & Career Guidance

Strategic post-school qualification direction and career pathway alignment

Target: Matriculants & Young Adults
Format: In-Person Paper Battery
Private Practice Rate (Itemised)

Navigating university degree choices, diploma options, and vocational routes requires a rigorous understanding of one's core cognitive profile and vocational affinity. This assessment provides structured, objective guidance to help matriculants and tertiary candidates choose university faculties, degrees, and career pathways with confidence.

Diagnostic Evaluation Dimensions

Vocational & Career Affinity

Multi-dimensional profiling across field categories, professional duties, and workplace environments.

Cognitive Profile & Analytical Abilities

In-depth testing of abstract thinking, critical reasoning, and academic competencies demanded by tertiary curricula.

Career Suitability & Pathway Fit

Direct synthesis of interests and aptitudes matched to South African university qualifications and professional fields.

Deliverables Included

  • Comprehensive paper-based psychometric testing battery
  • Formal norm-referenced scoring and occupational profile mapping
  • In-depth written career assessment report outlining viable fields of study and degree options
  • 1-on-1 in-person consultation reviewing results, strategic applications, and actionable next steps
Preparation Policy

No pre-assessment questionnaires or school reports are required beforehand.

Itemised invoice issued post-session for medical aid reimbursement submission.
